METHODOLOGY 2.0

Evidence, with boundaries.

This independent project observes ACP implementations. “Verified” applies to an individual observed operation under the recorded conditions. It is not certification of the whole agent or a measure of model quality.

We negotiate capabilities, create a minimal session, then exercise controlled scenarios. Unadvertised optional methods are left untested by default. The schema is pinned by its SHA-256; experimental surfaces may change independently.

Mock runs test protocol plumbing. They do not prove production login, provider availability or model quality. Real-provider runs and mock runs retain their own provenance.

Historical results keep their original timestamps and notes, and need a new run before they can use the current evidence labels. Records older than seven days are marked stale.
